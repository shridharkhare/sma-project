🔄 Simple Workflow

From Browse to Try-On
in Seconds

No more guessing. Set up once, try everything virtually across any shopping website.

01
👤

Create Your Digital Profile

Upload your photos (full body, upper body, face, feet, neck) once. The extension stores them securely for all future try-ons. Each photo type is used for different product categories.

02
🛍️

Browse Any Shopping Website

Visit Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, AJIO, Zara, or any other online store. The extension automatically detects products on the page using intelligent product detection algorithms.

03
🧩

Open Extension & Scan

Click the TryOn AI extension icon in your browser toolbar. Hit Scan to instantly detect all products on the current page — it works across product pages and listing pages.

04
👆

Select a Product

Pick any product from the detected list. The extension pulls the product image, title, price, and category automatically. You can also manually select the category for best results.

05
✨

Click Try On

Hit the glowing "Try On" button. Your profile photos and the product image are sent to the Gemini AI backend. The AI generates a photorealistic visualization of you wearing the product.

06
🎉

View & Save Your Result

See a realistic AI-generated image of you wearing the product! Save it, share it, or try another product. All results are stored in your try-on history for easy comparison.

⚡ Core Features

Everything You Need for
Virtual Shopping

Built with cutting-edge AI and thoughtful UX design for the ultimate shopping experience.

🌐

Works on Any Website

Intelligent multi-selector product detection using JSON-LD structured data, OpenGraph meta tags, and site-specific DOM selectors. Tested on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, AJIO, and more.

🤖

Gemini AI Generation

Powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash multimodal AI for photorealistic virtual try-on. Category-aware prompting ensures the right body part is shown with realistic draping and lighting.

👤

Personal Digital Profile

Upload 5 types of photos (full body, upper body, face, feet, neck) for different product categories. Set up once, reuse for all future try-ons with no repeated uploads.

🎨

Context-Aware Results

AI-generated backgrounds match the product style — a beach t-shirt appears in a beach setting, an evening dress in an elegant environment. Not just overlay, truly immersive visualization.

📱

Try-On History

Every try-on is saved locally. Review, compare, and revisit previous results. Build your personal virtual wardrobe to help make better purchase decisions over time.

🔒

Privacy First

Your profile photos are stored locally in Chrome's secure storage. They're only sent to our backend during try-on requests over HTTPS. No public exposure, no training usage without consent.

⚡

Real-Time Progress

Live progress bar and status messages keep you informed during AI generation. Cancel anytime. Failed requests are handled gracefully with clear error messages.

🏷️

Smart Category Detection

Automatically detects product category from title, description, and page structure. Manually override with the category selector for precise AI prompting and optimal results.

☁️

Vercel Backend

Serverless Node.js backend deployed on Vercel for global edge performance. API keys are securely stored as environment variables — never exposed in the extension code.

🚀 Get Started

Install & Start Shopping

Follow these steps to get the TryOn AI Chrome Extension running locally.

1

Deploy the Backend to Vercel

Navigate to the backend/ folder. Set up a Vercel project, add your GEMINI_API_KEY as an environment variable, and deploy. Copy your deployment URL (e.g., https://your-project.vercel.app).

2

Update Backend URL in Extension

Open extension/popup.js and update the BACKEND_URL constant at the top with your Vercel deployment URL.

3

Load Extension in Chrome

Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions. Enable "Developer Mode" (top right toggle). Click "Load Unpacked" and select the extension/ folder from this project.

4

Set Up Your Digital Profile

Click the TryOn AI extension icon (pin it first if needed). Click the 👤 profile icon and upload your photos. At minimum, upload a full-body or upper-body photo to get started.

5

Browse & Try On!

Navigate to any shopping website (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.). Open the extension, click Scan, select a product, and hit "Try On"! The AI will generate your personalized try-on result.
